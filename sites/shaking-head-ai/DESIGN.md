# Shaking Head AI

A playful photo booth for one expressive head-shake video. Warm butter-yellow background, deep navy ink, sunflower-yellow action buttons, a few cream surfaces. Thick dark outlines and offset shadows give it a handmade photo-booth feel. Rounded, substantial typography; editorial headlines paired with compact controls. No gradients, fake social proof, countdowns or unnecessary badges.

The first screen contains the generator: upload on the left, a generous portrait stage on the right. On mobile the upload and action precede the preview. Only animal subjects are supported: no selfies, human examples or person categories. One locked head-shake effect, one animal photo, two clear duration prices. Three credited original TikTok references sit beside the upload form; they are not advertised as our outputs. Below them, reviewed generated examples pair their clickable input photo with the actual generated video. Show category tabs only when more than one real example exists; switch the same photo-to-video stage and never show placeholder results. Keep the original TikTok references and the generated sample visually and factually distinct. Both use quiet play/pause and sound controls.

Keep the site's native login, account, checkout and generation services. Restore the uploaded photo and selected duration across login, payment and refresh. Respect reduced motion and keyboard focus. Show errors inline; do not silently spend a second time. Display simulation status only in a test environment.

Desktop photo upload also offers “Upload from phone”, adapted from the repository's existing QR handoff. The QR pairs one photo for 30 minutes and never starts a generation. Close or expiry cancels the session; a new desktop photo supersedes an older pairing. Phones use a single-purpose upload page with no marketing navigation, analytics or Google One Tap. Both paths prepare the same 720 × 1280 JPEG portrait.

Show the actual minimum credit-pack payment ($8.80) beside the sign-in requirement. The duration selector and generate button carry the generation's credit cost. Explain once that generating with a sample photo also uses credits; viewing examples does not. Keep audio among the ordinary video specifications.

# Shaking Head AI

A playful photo booth for one expressive head-shake video. Warm butter-yellow background, deep navy ink, sunflower-yellow action buttons, a few cream surfaces. Thick dark outlines and offset shadows give it a handmade photo-booth feel. Rounded, substantial typography; editorial headlines paired with compact controls. No gradients, fake social proof, countdowns or unnecessary badges.

The first screen contains the generator: upload on the left, a generous portrait stage on the right. On mobile the upload and action precede the preview. One locked head-shake effect, one photo, one clear price. Three credited original TikTok references sit beside the upload form; they are not advertised as our outputs. Examples show input photos and are described as such until real generated samples have been reviewed.

Keep the site's native login, account, checkout and generation services. Restore the uploaded photo and selected mood across login, payment and refresh. Respect reduced motion and keyboard focus. Show errors inline; do not silently spend a second time. Display simulation status only in a test environment.
